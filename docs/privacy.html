Liquiflux for Roku: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Liquiflux is a player for the movies and shows on your own Plex Media Server. This policy explains what the Liquiflux app for Roku handles and where it goes. In short: Liquiflux has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, shows no ads and sells nothing.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • To plex.tv, to sign you in: the email and password you type on the TV, and a two-step code if your account uses one. Plex returns a sign-in token. The app then asks plex.tv for your Plex Home profiles and for the servers your account can reach.
  • To your own Plex Media Server, to browse and play: requests for your libraries and artwork, playback requests, and progress updates so the server knows where you stopped. If you change audio or subtitles, or search for subtitles, that request goes to your server too, and your server handles it.

Your password is sent only to plex.tv, at sign-in, and is never stored by the app. Nothing is sent to the developer of Liquiflux or to anyone else.

What the app keeps on your Roku

To keep you signed in, the app stores on the Roku device itself: the sign-in tokens Plex gave it, the chosen profile and server (name and address), and a random identifier for this installation that Plex uses to tell your devices apart. It also remembers a short list of titles that needed a converted picture, so they start faster next time. Signing out, or removing the app, deletes this.

What others collect

Plex and Roku have their own privacy policies, which apply to your Plex account and your Roku device. The app reports the standard launch and playback-start signals that Roku asks every app to send, which Roku uses to measure performance.

Children

Liquiflux is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@liquiflux.app.

Liquiflux is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Plex or Roku.
